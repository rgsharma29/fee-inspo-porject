About Sideburn

A modern library management system built with passion.

Sideburn is a student-created library management system designed to make managing books, borrowers, returns, and fines simple and visually engaging. We abandoned boring tables and plain designs in favor of a creative, Gen-Z oriented aesthetic.

All team members contributed to the idea and development of this project.
